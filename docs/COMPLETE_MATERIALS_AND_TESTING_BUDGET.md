# Complete device, calibration, and testing budget checklist

Prepared: 5 November 2026. Currency: Philippine pesos. Scope: one screenless ESP32 breadboard prototype, bounded onboard-flash recording, subsequent Wi-Fi upload, and web monitoring/advisory development.

This is a costing checklist, not a finalized wiring plan, supplier quotation, or approved sample-size plan. Exact hardware, power design, calibration methods, and sample counts remain to be selected with the freelancer and adviser. Stock is not reserved.

## 1. Can the Kaggle dataset remain the final training source?

Yes. Public data can remain the training source for the finished prototype when its license, inputs, labels, and intended use are appropriate. It does not have to be replaced simply because it came from Kaggle.

The specific [Predicting Optimal Fertilizers competition](https://www.kaggle.com/c/playground-series-s5e6/data) lists CC0 and explains that its train/test data were generated by a deep-learning model. Its target is `Fertilizer Name`. [CC0 permits copying, modification, and distribution, including commercial use](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Verify the exact dataset/version; other Kaggle uploads have different terms. Preserve its citation and provenance for academic reporting.

Before connecting a trained model to the device:

- Match each feature's meaning and unit to an available input. Soil temperature cannot automatically substitute for air temperature, and soil moisture cannot substitute for air humidity.
- Confirm the nutrient fractions and analysis methods. Matching N, P, K column names or converting units does not establish equivalence to the probe.
- Decide whether the desired output is a fertilizer name or a compatibility class. A dataset labeled with fertilizer names does not directly establish new compatibility-class labels.
- Keep unsupported features unavailable; do not invent missing field values to make predictions run.
- Report benchmark performance on the external data separately from independent local/reference testing. Synthetic-data accuracy does not demonstrate crop response or local agronomic effectiveness.
- If labels are generated from documented rules, assess agreement with those rules and report that origin. Such agreement alone is not evidence of yield improvement.

Public training data can reduce original training-data collection work. They do not remove the hardware calibration and reference comparisons already proposed in the paper. A local validation set can be held out entirely from training.

Agree the soil sources, independent samples, replicates, preparation methods, nutrient fractions, and acceptance criteria with the adviser/laboratory before commissioning analysis. Repeated probe readings from one sample are not automatically independent training or validation samples.

For the **995/sample** example: 10 submissions = **9,950**; 20 = **19,900**; 30 = **29,850**. These illustrate arithmetic only. They are not recommended or statistically justified sample counts, and the laboratory may require additional charges. A future dataset-collection program can cost more than the prototype hardware.

[DA Region III publishes a student-discount policy](https://rfo3.da.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/pages/RSL/downloadables/Guidelines%20for%20laboratory%20fees.pdf). That does not establish your eligibility or the fees at your selected laboratory. Ask your university and DA Region II/selected lab for a written quotation and any applicable student assistance before entering a discount in the budget.

**Actual fertilizer products, seeds, pots, growing medium, irrigation equipment, and crop-trial space are only needed if an actual application/growth trial is added.** The current decision-support prototype does not by itself require a crop-response experiment. Checking fertilizer composition from documented references does not require buying every fertilizer. Budget an expanded trial separately if the adviser requires one.

At 20 such submissions the illustrative total is **55,214**; at 30 it is **65,164**. These are scenario totals, not quotations or recommended sample plans. Additional samples may also change bags, buffers, travel, delivery, and repeat-test costs; update those allowances rather than assuming they stay fixed.

The earlier approximately 17,000 materials budget included a smaller consumables allowance and excluded lab validation and travel. **Do not add that entire earlier budget to this one**; the categories overlap. This expanded example replaces that scenario and shows more of the testing-related costs.

Overall formula:

```text
Overall project cost =
  actual device/assembly purchases
  + actual calibration/sample supplies
  + tool/reference access or purchases
  + laboratory charges for the approved plan
  + development/other labor not already included
  + data/hosting/domain costs
  + delivery/travel/evaluation/submission costs
  + any justified replacement reserve
```

Track combined project cost separately from personal cash outlay. The user previously clarified that materials are purchased by the user and reimbursed by the freelancer, separately from the 15,000 development fee. Confirm whether that reimbursement agreement also includes consumables, lab fees, travel, delivery, and replacements; do not assume it does.
